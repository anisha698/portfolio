Questions people usually ask me

What kind of roles are you looking for?

I’m looking for full-time roles across visual design, brand design, product design, and design engineering—especially where I can shape the idea, system, and final experience.

Are you a visual/brand designer or a design engineer?

Both. I bring a visual designer’s eye to the build, staying close enough to the code to protect what made the idea special—typography, interaction, motion, and how the experience actually behaves.

I’m based in the NYC/NJ area, in Jersey City with an easy commute to Manhattan. I’m also open to relocating anywhere in the U.S. for the right opportunity.
