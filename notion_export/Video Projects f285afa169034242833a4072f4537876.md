## Assignment Project for OnJuno:-

### Project Brief:

OnJuno is launching trading of Solana (SOL) inside the app. The task was to design creatives across email and social media to announce the launch to new and existing users.
Part 1 - This part was to design a twitter post announcing the launch of Solana coin (SOL) trading within the OnJuno app to our social media followers.
The following are the 3 points to be communicated,
Users can buy and sell Solana (SOL) at zero fees
They can send and receive Solana to and from any phantom wallet
Users can also earn 10% interest on Solana if they store it in their wallets.
