Three beats, one announcement

OnJuno was launching Solana (SOL) trading inside the app. The task was to design creatives across email and social to announce the launch to new and existing users.

Three things to communicate:

  • Users can buy and sell Solana at zero fees.
  • Send and receive SOL to and from any Phantom wallet.
  • Earn 10% interest on SOL stored in-wallet.

I started with a Twitter-first storyboard, quick hook, three clear beats, confident close. The final animation kept pacing tight so the value props landed without feeling dense.
