Building trust through visuals in the booking flow

A set of trust icons to communicate credibility and reassurance at key decision-making moments in Headout's booking flow, designed to feel clear, intuitive, and brand-aligned.

ClientHeadout
RoleVisual design · Illustration
Year2021
Timeline2 weeks
01 / 03

Where trust needed to show up

Headout needed a set of icons for the trust section of its webpage to establish credibility and reassurance in its booking process. These icons began at the bottom of the page, but were later moved up to become an integral part of the booking flow at key decision moments.

The brief: design a set of trust icons that visually communicate credibility and assurance, enhancing user confidence without feeling decorative or generic.

02 / 03

From abstract to human

I began by listing the trust factors that needed visual representation, 1M+ happy customers, reliable support, secure payments, verified experiences. Research into competitor approaches helped refine the direction.

Iconography explorations ran on a spectrum, minimalist vs. detailed, abstract vs. literal. Through feedback, I landed on a metaphor-led approach: characters, expressions, and small narratives that felt human rather than corporate.

03 / 03

The insight that steered the set

The "1M+ happy customers" icon was pivotal in setting the direction. Emotional resonance, love and satisfaction, communicated trust more effectively than numerical proof ever could. Every other icon in the set followed that thread.
